How it works

Three steps. No scrolling through hundreds of posts.

1

Ask for blood

Post the blood type, units, hospital and urgency. The patient's name stays private.

2

Smart matching

Only donors with a compatible type, within range, who waited 56 days since their last donation can see it.

3

Pledge & confirm

A donor pledges, the requester confirms the donation, and the request closes when enough units arrive.
